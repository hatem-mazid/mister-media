# Mister Media — Project Definition

## 1. Project Overview

**Mister Media** is an established creative and marketing agency based in Turkey, with **more than 10 years of experience** serving clients across multiple countries.

The agency combines creative design, branding, printing, packaging, digital marketing, video production, web design, and exhibition-related services.

The new website will present Mister Media as a professional, capable, and creative agency with a strong portfolio, an established client base, and a proven track record built over more than a decade.

The website should communicate that Mister Media can take an idea from **creation to execution and delivery**.

---

## 2. Project Vision

The website is part of a new digital direction for Mister Media.

With more than 10 years of industry experience, the agency has developed a broad range of creative and marketing capabilities. The new direction places greater emphasis on:

* Printing
* Packaging
* Graphic design
* Branding and visual identity
* High-quality physical brand execution

The website should therefore preserve the agency's full-service capabilities while giving stronger visibility to **printing and packaging**.

The positioning should not make Mister Media appear to be only a printing company. Instead, it should present the agency as an experienced creative and marketing partner capable of managing projects from concept through final production.

---

## 3. Current Positioning

Mister Media describes itself as:

> Artists in Graphic Design, Experts in Printing Solution, and Specialized in E-Marketing.

The agency's communication frequently focuses on transforming ideas into strong visual communication and helping brands create a consistent presence across different touchpoints.

With more than 10 years of experience, Mister Media brings established knowledge, practical expertise, and a proven understanding of creative production and brand execution.

Core idea:

**A strong idea needs strong execution to reach people.**

Mister Media aims to take a client's idea and turn it into creative, professional, and effective visual communication.
